## 1. Business overview — what we do

Good AI Australia provides affordable, fully-managed AI voice agents to small and medium enterprises (SMEs) in Perth, Western Australia.

A Good AI voice agent answers a business's phone around the clock. It handles enquiries, books appointments, answers frequently asked questions from the business's own knowledge base, takes messages, and transfers callers to a person when the conversation needs one. It speaks in a natural, warm, human voice — not as a robotic phone menu.

We deliver this as a *productised service*, not a custom software project. The business runs on a small set of pre-built, proven voice-agent templates that we configure to each client. This "build once, deploy many" model lets us stand up a working voice agent in roughly one hour of founder time and have it live within 48 hours of the client supplying their details — at a fraction of the cost and wait of a custom-developed agent.

Clients pay a one-time setup fee plus a fixed monthly subscription scaled to their expected call volume. That recurring revenue is supplemented by custom voice design, paid digital health checks, custom development, and hourly prompt-engineering consulting.

The offering at launch is organised into seven subscription tiers — from a $149/month Business Starter agent for sole traders up to a $499/month fully customised agent — plus add-on services. The same productised stack is also positioned for **white-label resale to other agencies and consultants** (an emerging route to market enabled by the underlying platform's multi-tenant capability). Tier detail and pricing sit in Sections 3 and 11.

The business operates as a sole trader, founded and run by Kevin Tan, and is being established with support from the Self-Employment Assistance (SEA) program.

## 3. Unique selling point — features & benefits

Good AI Australia occupies the middle ground that the Perth market currently leaves empty. Today an SME has only two realistic options: pay a digital agency $5,000–$15,000 and wait 8–12 weeks for a custom-built voice agent, or wrestle with a do-it-yourself developer platform that demands technical skill the owner does not have. Good AI is the only local provider offering a *managed implementation* — the affordability of a software subscription with the hands-on setup and support of a local agency.

**The unique selling point:** enterprise-grade AI voice capability at startup pricing, delivered through an efficient, template-based model, with privacy compliance built in and a real Perth-based person behind it.

The value proposition rests on five pillars:

1. **Affordability.** By removing the bulk of custom development time, setup fees are 85–93% below custom development ($5,000–$15,000) and monthly subscriptions are 50–75% below typical local agency retainers ($300–$1,000). Subscriptions run from $149/month for micro-businesses to $499/month for a fully customised agent.
2. **Speed.** A working agent is deployed within 48 hours of the client supplying their business information — the client sees a return almost immediately.
3. **Simplicity.** Clients need no technical knowledge. They choose a template, complete a short onboarding form, and the rest — including connection to their existing phone number — is handled for them.
4. **Local support.** Unlike faceless overseas DIY platforms, clients have a direct line to a Perth-based expert for support, questions and changes, including ongoing prompt optimisation.
5. **Compliance, today (not future-proofing).** Solutions run on a platform that already holds SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR and TCPA certifications, with configurable Australian data residency. PRIS Act alignment is therefore a *current certified posture*, not a stated intention — a position no other Perth SME-focused competitor can match without spending months on accreditation.

**Headline differentiators:** PRIS-aligned by design; high-definition voice quality on Australian infrastructure; and a "grant-ready" solution aligned with WA government digital grants so clients can part-fund their adoption. The simple ownership question we put to every prospect: *$149–$499 a month for a 24/7 assistant — or $60,000+ a year for a receptionist with annual leave and sick days?*

**Agent capability model (Verify · Ground · Act · Follow-through).** Trillet agents do four things on every call: (1) **verify** the caller via 2FA, voice biometrics, or CRM identity check; (2) **ground** responses in live business data — account history, open tickets, policies, calendar availability — not stale extracts; (3) **act** inside connected systems during the call (book appointments, create CRM records, raise tickets, send SMS confirmations, send calendar invites); and (4) **follow through** after the call ends with confirmations, next-best-action records, and escalation routing to the right human. This is what makes the offering substantively different from a simple "answering service" — the call doesn't end at a message; it ends with the work done.

## 8. Operations & service delivery + weekly work plan

### 8.1 Service delivery model

The operational cornerstone of Good AI Australia is a standardised, repeatable delivery model that brings a fully functional voice agent live in roughly **one hour of founder time** and within **48 hours** of the client providing their information. Delivery runs in three stages:

1. **Client onboarding — 15 minutes of client time, automated.** On selecting a template, the client receives an automated welcome email containing a link to a Google Form (hours, departments, key contacts, call-routing rules) and a link to a 10-minute video tutorial. The client also provides their website URL — Trillet's AI builder reads the site automatically to seed the agent's understanding of services, pricing, hours and FAQs, removing most of the manual configuration the previous build process required.
2. **Founder configuration — 30 minutes of founder time.** The founder clones the chosen proprietary template in the platform, embeds the client's details into the agent's knowledge base, and runs three test calls to confirm functionality before sending a review link to the client.
3. **Activation call — 30 minutes of founder time.** A live call connects the client's existing business number to the agent, walks the client through their dashboard (call logs, messages), and ends with a final live test call to confirm satisfaction before the service goes formally active.

This "build once, deploy many" approach is what lets Good AI undercut the time and cost of custom development, which typically requires weeks of discovery, build and testing per client. Because each deployment consumes only about an hour of founder time, **delivery capacity is not the constraint in Year 1 — sales and onboarding throughput is.** At a conservative one to two deployments per delivery block, the weekly schedule below comfortably supports the Phase 2–3 client ramp without a second pair of hands until the Month 6 virtual assistant is added.
