**Attracts**: Established professionals and family offices seeking a trusted, heritage-feel advisory relationship.

## Technical Notes

- All palettes pass **WCAG AAA** for body text (primary on neutral) — well above the AA 4.5:1 minimum
- All fonts are **Google Fonts** — compatible with Elementor and web implementation
- Accent colours marked "decorative only" should not be used for text smaller than 24px
- No stock photos included per brief — mood image directions provided for future sourcing
- LM monogram design is not modified — only recolouring described
