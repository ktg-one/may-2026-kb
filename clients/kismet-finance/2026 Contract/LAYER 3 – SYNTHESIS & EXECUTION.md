### **1. LOCAL CAPABILITY FUND: DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION ROUND 2025–26**

**Owner / Program body:** Department of Energy and Economic Diversification (WA Government)

**Scope:** WA statewide; SMEs operating in or relocating to WA

**What it funds (digital focus):**

- Digital systems implementation (ERP, CRM, job management, accounting software)
- eCommerce and digital presence (website, payment systems, online ordering)
- Automation \& workflow (RPA, process automation, inventory management)
- Data \& BI (dashboards, reporting, analytics platforms)
- Cybersecurity uplift (cloud security, access controls, backups, staff training)

**Funding amount / co-contribution:**

- **Stream 1:** Up to \$25,000 grant (requires 50% co-contribution = min. \$25k total project spend)
- **Stream 2:** Up to \$50,000 grant (requires 50% co-contribution = min. \$100k total project spend)
- Co-contribution can include in-kind labour, existing systems, or cash

**Who can apply (SME definition if stated):**

- Turnover: AUD \$1M–\$50M annually
- Employees: Not explicitly limited; typically 5–200 FTE
- Must be WA-based or planning to establish in WA

**Status:**

- **Open** – Round 1 (2025–26) closed December 2025; Round 2 (2026–27) timeline [unknown] but historically annual
- Last updated: 16 January 2026

**Official link(s):**

- https://www.wa.gov.au/government/publications/local-capability-fund-digital-transformation-round
- https://www.wa.gov.au/system/files/2025-07/digital-transformation-round-guidelines-2025-26_0.pdf
- Application portal: https://lcf.smartygrants.com.au

***

**Mandatory eligibility gates (tick all before applying):**

- [ ] ABN registered and operating in WA (or WA project address confirmed)
- [ ] Turnover AUD \$1M–\$50M in most recent financial year
- [ ] Not received an LCF Digital Transformation grant in the last 3 years
- [ ] Project focused on operational improvement and/or advanced technology (not R\&D or capital equipment only)
- [ ] Digital/tech/cyber/data scope confirmed (not purely marketing or general admin)
- [ ] Co-funding secured or cashflow plan documented (50% of eligible project costs)
- [ ] Vendor quotes obtained and eligible cost mapping complete
- [ ] Project timeline achievable within 12–18 months delivery window
- [ ] Directors/owners free from conflict of interest or insolvency

**Assessment / merit criteria (scoring, not strict gates):**

- Innovation and competitiveness of digital solution (20%)
- Business growth, efficiency, or capability uplift impact (20%)
- Value for money and co-investment confidence (15%)
- Digital maturity and readiness to implement (15%)
- Regional benefit or priority sector alignment (10%)
- Risk management and cyber/privacy considerations (10%)
- Job creation or retention (10%)

**Evidence pack to prepare (minimum, LCF-specific):**

- [ ] 2–3 page project plan: current state → digital gap → solution → expected outcomes → timeline
- [ ] Budget spreadsheet: eligible costs (labour, software licenses, cloud services, integration, training) + co-contribution breakdown
- [ ] 2–3 supplier quotes matching scope, timelines, and eligible cost categories
- [ ] ABN confirmation, financials (last 2 years P\&L, turnover verification)
- [ ] Cyber/risk assessment (even brief): data classification, backup strategy, access controls, staff training plan
- [ ] Outcome metrics: baseline KPIs (e.g. order-to-cash time, error rate, staff hours saved) and 12-month targets
- [ ] Conflict of interest declaration (if required)

**Best-fit SME profiles (examples):**

- **Manufacturing SME (10–40 FTE):** Implementing ERP to replace spreadsheet ordering + production planning; seeking \$30–50k grant to fund system, implementation, staff training.
- **Professional services firm (15–50 FTE):** Moving to cloud CRM and document automation; \$20–40k grant to cover software, integration, and change management.
- **Retail/F\&B SME with 2–3 locations:** Adding eCommerce + POS integration + inventory sync; \$15–30k grant for platform, payment gateway, multi-location setup.
- **Trades/construction SME:** Implementing job costing, field mobile app, and invoicing; \$25–40k grant for software, mobile devices, and staff training.

**Scoring (1–5):**

- Digital relevance: **5/5** – core WA program, explicitly digital-focused
- Ease of eligibility: **4/5** – clear SME definition, straightforward gates, but turnover window (\$1M–\$50M) excludes micro-SMEs
- Speed to apply: **4/5** – ~8–10 weeks from project plan to submission; moderate documentation
- Likelihood of approval: **3.5/5** – competitive (previous rounds 20–40% approval); merit-based assessment; cyber/risk is a scoring factor

### **3. R\&D TAX INCENTIVE (SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT)**

**Owner / Program body:** Australian Taxation Office (ATO), administered via tax return

**Scope:** National; applies to SMEs with R\&D activities in eligible fields (incl. software, digital innovation)

**What it funds (digital focus):**

- Software development for new products/features (not customisation of off-shelf packages)
- Data analytics and BI tool development
- Automation and AI-adjacent ML/algorithm development
- Cybersecurity research (developing security frameworks, not just implementing off-shelf tools)
- **NOT eligible:** routine IT support, standard web design, implementation of existing software

**Funding amount / co-contribution:**

- Tax offset or refund, not a cash grant:
    - SMEs (turnover <\$20M): up to 43.5% R\&D tax offset (refundable)
    - Larger companies: up to 38.5% (non-refundable)
- Applies to actual R\&D spend (labour, contractors, materials, IP licensing)

**Who can apply (SME definition if stated):**

- Turnover: <\$20M annually (SME definition)
- Must be Australian-registered entity
- R\&D activities must meet ATO's "core" or "supporting" R\&D test

**Status:**

- **Ongoing** – ATO program, annual claims via tax return
- Eligibility recently clarified (2024–25) for software, AI/ML activities
- Last updated: 16 January 2026

**Official link(s):**

- https://www.ato.gov.au/Business/Research-and-development-tax-incentive
- https://www.ato.gov.au/general/research-and-development-tax-incentive/eligibility

***

**Mandatory eligibility gates (tick all before applying):**

- [ ] Australian company or eligible entity with ABN
- [ ] Turnover <\$20M in income year (SME threshold)
- [ ] R\&D activities are "core" (driving toward new knowledge/innovation) or "supporting" (necessary to core)
- [ ] Can substantiate R\&D spend (payroll, contractor invoices, materials) for the year
- [ ] Not claiming same activities under other grants (overlap check)
- [ ] No disqualifying conduct (fraud, tax evasion history)

**Assessment / merit criteria (N/A – objective eligibility test):**

- ATO assessor reviews project scope, methodology, novelty claim
- Must pass "core R\&D test": is it advancing the field of knowledge in digital/tech?

**Evidence pack to prepare (minimum, ATO-specific):**

- [ ] Project summary (1–2 pages): problem → R\&D question → methodology → outcomes; focus on uncertainty/novelty
- [ ] List of R\&D personnel and time allocation (%) to R\&D vs non-R\&D work
- [ ] Payroll records or contractor invoices (labour costs)
- [ ] Receipts/invoices for materials, software, tools, external R\&D services
- [ ] Project diary or lab notes (evidence of development process, not just outcomes)
- [ ] Correspondence with ATO or tax advisor (optional but recommended; IP Australia search if patenting)
- [ ] Reconciliation of R\&D spend to tax return

**Best-fit SME profiles (examples):**

- **SaaS/software startup (5–20 FTE):** Developing proprietary BI dashboard with custom ML for predictive analytics; \$150k–500k R\&D spend → \$65–218k tax offset.
- **Digital agency (10–30 FTE):** Building proprietary content automation tool using API integration; \$80–200k R\&D spend → \$35–87k offset.
- **Manufacturing SME (20–50 FTE):** Custom IoT sensor system for production monitoring (hardware + embedded software); software R\&D portion \$100–300k → offset.
- **Fintech SME (10–25 FTE):** Developing cybersecurity protocol for payment processing; \$200–500k R\&D spend → \$87–218k offset.

**Scoring (1–5):**

- Digital relevance: **4/5** – highly relevant for software/digital innovation; excludes non-R\&D implementation
- Ease of eligibility: **3/5** – objective test but requires clear R\&D definition; ATO scrutiny on "novelty"; needs professional advice
- Speed to apply: **2/5** – requires annual accounting and tax filing; claims lodged with tax return (12–18 months after year-end)
- Likelihood of approval: **4/5** – if project meets R\&D test, offset is granted; audit risk if claim is vague

**⚠️ Key distinction:** This is a **tax incentive**, not a grant. SME can only "claim" after incurring R\&D spend; cash return typically received months after tax filing. Not suitable for immediate project funding but complements post-project cashflow.

# B) CROSS-GRANT "UNIVERSAL ELIGIBILITY" CHECKLIST

### **One-Page SME Pre-Grant Readiness Checklist**

Use this checklist **before pursuing ANY WA digital grant** to confirm you are ready.

***

#### **Business Eligibility**

- [ ] ABN registered and active
- [ ] Operating in Western Australia (or WA project address confirmed)
- [ ] Meet SME size threshold (turnover / employees per grant – see grant cards for specific limits)
- [ ] No disqualifying previous grant receipts (e.g. same grant program within last 2–3 years)
- [ ] Business operating for minimum 12 months (where required)


#### **Financial Readiness**

- [ ] Turnover / financials auditable (last 2 years P\&L or tax returns available)
- [ ] Cashflow to cover co-contribution / matched spend (if grant requires 50% match)
- [ ] No intention to claim same costs under other grants (overlap check done)
- [ ] Able to cashflow project costs before reimbursement (grants typically reimburse 4–8 weeks after submission)
- [ ] Current tax compliance (no outstanding ATO or ASIC issues)


#### **Governance \& Compliance**

- [ ] Director / owner authority confirmed (can sign grant agreements)
- [ ] Company insurances current (public liability, workers comp where applicable; cyber [unknown] if required by specific grant)
- [ ] No conflict of interest (directors/relatives not suppliers or assessors)
- [ ] No insolvency proceedings or significant legal disputes


#### **Digital Project Readiness**

- [ ] Project scope defined (what problem, what digital solution, what outcomes)
- [ ] Digital vendor(s) shortlisted; able to obtain 2–3 compliant quotes
- [ ] Implementation timeline realistic (12–18 months typical for LCF; 8–12 weeks for SBDC digital marketing)
- [ ] Cyber/risk considerations mapped (data classification, backup, access controls, staff training)


#### **Measurement \& Acquittal Readiness**

- [ ] Baseline metrics captured (current state: order-to-cash time, error rate, staff hours, revenue, customer count, etc.)
- [ ] 12-month outcome KPIs defined (what will improve by how much)
- [ ] Simple data collection plan (systems, dashboards, or reports to track progress)
- [ ] Ability to retain all invoices, receipts, timesheets, quotes for audit (typically 5+ years)
- [ ] Designated staff member to manage reporting and acquittal
