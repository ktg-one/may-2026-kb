02 / DOMAINS

Five problem domains. Two stated. Three underneath.

The two stated asks — automation and a centralised intranet — are real. But they're symptoms. The three domains underneath them (knowledge, talent, QA) are what generate the compounding return. Each is buildable in isolation; together they form a single operating layer.

01
Lead wedge · most underestimated

Engineering Knowledge Bot

70+ engineers across four sites manage 100+ concurrent NPI projects. The "how" of complex tri-layer co-extrusion, PEBAX/PEEK rheology, and PTFE etching parameters lives in heads, not systems. Every senior departure stretches NPI cycles. Every ramp at Bac Ninh waits on knowledge transfer that today moves at the speed of flights.

Architecture
  • Ingest layer. Closed-LLM RAG over SOPs, NPI reports, validation protocols, Slack/Teams threads, drawing repos, MES exports, deviation logs.
  • Parameter card extraction. Every NPI run yields one structured card — material lot, die geometry, melt profile, line speed, defect signature, fix.
  • Role-gated retrieval. Engineers get parameter recall; QA gets deviation precedent; sales-engineering gets tolerance feasibility live during OEM calls.
  • QMSR-ready governance. Every response logged with sources, model card, drift monitor, human-in-the-loop sign-off for regulated submissions.
DAY 1—30
Ingest 90% of Singapore corpus. Private RAG to 5 senior engineers. Measure recall on 50 known-answer questions.
DAY 31—60
Extend to Xiamen + Irvine. Slack/Teams integration. Parameter card extraction live.
DAY 61—90
Bac Ninh rollout (timed with ISO cert). Audit trail hardened. QA incident replay live.
15–25% NPI cycle reduction on second-instance projects · one avoided 12-month revalidation pays the engagement many times over.
02
Sensitive · founder-owned · external-first

Talent Intelligence & Retention Signal

Replacing a senior process engineer in Singapore costs 150–300% of salary plus 6–18 months of AP-specific ramp. The risk vector is invisible until exit. The fix is an early-warning system that runs founder-direct, never through middle management, and is defensible if leaked.

Architecture
  • External signal layer. LinkedIn activity deltas, recruiter-connection bursts, conference roster mentions, sector job-posting velocity.
  • Internal signal layer (consent-based). HR pulse integration, 1:1 cadence drift, project-rotation gaps, knowledge-bot usage drop-off as disengagement proxy.
  • Per-engineer composite score. Weekly digest to Charles & Sean only. Individual scores never persisted — only the intervention recommendation is stored.
  • Stay conversation playbook. Pre-built career-track scoping, equity refresh signal, structured intervention. Designed by you, run by the founder.
DAY 1—30
External-only MVP on the 20 most critical engineers. Weekly digest live.
DAY 31—60
Optional internal signal integration. Manager 1:1 cadence tracking.
DAY 61—90
Intervention playbook live. Retention KPI dashboard. Quarterly retention review cadence.
One avoided senior departure ≈ S$300–500K loaded · target 3+/yr retained = S$1M+ & protected NPI velocity.
03
Compliance wedge becomes commercial moat

AI-Native Quality Assurance

Today: CpK 1.33, 4-sigma, ~63 PPM. The frontier: CpK 1.67+, <1 PPM. Manual visual inspection at 0.0005" wall and 0.01" PTFE ID is mathematically impossible. Add QMSR (Feb 2026), and AI in the production loop now needs validation, audit trails, drift monitoring. Most CDMOs are scrambling. Build it as a sales asset instead.

Architecture
  • Vision layer. Line-scan + laser micrometry. AI defect classifier with explainability overlays for QA reviewers.
  • Closed-loop control. Reinforcement-learning agent for extrusion parameters within pre-validated guardrails. Temperature, pressure, pull-speed.
  • PINN module. Physics-informed neural net for reflow bonding thermal profile — delamination prevention by design.
  • QMSR governance wrapper. Model card, validation per ISO 13485:7.5.6 + CSA, full inference audit trail, drift monitor with HITL trigger. This is the differentiator.
  • Yield dashboard. Per-site, per-line, per-material-lot. Becomes the spine of the centralised intranet.
DAY 1—30
One Singapore extrusion line. Vision in shadow mode for 2 weeks. Measure agreement vs human.
DAY 31—60
Advisory mode live. QMSR governance wrapper piloted with Sean's QMS team.
DAY 61—90
Closed-loop on one parameter (line speed first). Begin Xiamen replication.
+40% defect capture · −50% inspection time · S$90–180K/yr scrap prevented per line · QMSR posture as RFP win factor.

Centralised Intranet — Three Pillars, One Brief

Charles wants insight, not a dashboard farm. Build three pillars and one weekly LLM-narrated brief delivered to his phone Monday 7am SGT. He doesn't open dashboards; he reads a paragraph that tells him what changed, what's anomalous, what to act on.

Architecture
  • Foundation. Lightweight semantic layer over a unified analytical store (Snowflake or BigQuery). dbt or Cube.dev for the layer.
  • Pillar 1 — OEM Pipeline & NPI Velocity. Projects by stage, days-in-stage, parameter-card completeness, blocker map.
  • Pillar 2 — Operational Health. Yield, scrap, downtime, CpK trend by site / line / material.
  • Pillar 3 — Talent & Knowledge. Engineer utilisation, knowledge-bot usage, retention risk score (ties to Domain 02).
  • Monday Brief. LLM-generated narrative — what changed, what's anomalous, what to act on. Phone-readable. Audited weekly for accuracy.

DAY 31—60
Pillars 2 & 3 live.

03 / ROADMAP

A 90-day rollout that ships, not deliberates.

Each phase ends with something running in production, not a slide deck. Phase 0 is a low-commitment fit test. Phase 1 onward is build. Every phase has a measurable deliverable and a kill-switch decision point.

Phase 0 · Discovery
Weeks 1–2. Site visit (Singapore), data audit, stakeholder interviews with Charles, Sean, Russell, 3 senior engineers, QMS lead. Output: written diagnostic, prioritised domain shortlist, Phase 1 scope. Kill-switch: at end of Week 2 either side can walk.
Phase 1 · Foundation
Days 1–30. Knowledge Bot v1 (Singapore), Top-3 workflows, OEM Pipeline dashboard. Output: three live systems, one usage metric per system, written hand-off doc.
Phase 2 · Expansion
Days 31–60. All five domains in production at Singapore. QA shadow mode, Talent signal external-only, full top-10 automation, all three intranet pillars. Output: every domain has a Singapore reference implementation.
Phase 3 · Multi-site
Days 61–90. Xiamen + Bac Ninh + Irvine extension. Bac Ninh sequencing aligned to ISO 13485 cert. Output: knowledge bot multi-site, QA in advisory mode at 2 sites, Monday Brief live.
Steady State
Day 91+. Optional retainer or transition to internal team. All systems source-controlled, documented, handover-ready from Day 1. No lock-in by design.
