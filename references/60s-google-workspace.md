# 60-Second Training Scripts — Google Workspace

Target: Kismet field consultants. They use Gmail & Calendar but barely scratch the surface.
Format: 60 seconds max. Screen recording + voiceover.
Rule: One trick per day. Things they'll actually use in the field.

---

## WEEK 1: GMAIL — "Stop drowning in email"

### Day 1: Stars & Priority
> **Hook:** "You have 200 emails. Only 5 matter. Here's how to find them."
>
> Open Gmail. See an important email? Click the star on the left.
> Now click "Starred" in the sidebar.
> That's your VIP list. Star client emails, star Shane's emails.
> Everything else can wait. Check Starred first every morning.
>
> **CTA:** "Tomorrow — templates so you stop retyping the same email."

### Day 2: Email Templates
> **Hook:** "You type the same follow-up email 10 times a week. Stop."
>
> Compose a new email. Click the three dots at the bottom right.
> Click "Templates" > "Save draft as template".
> Name it "Follow-up after DQ" or "Discovery confirmation".
> Next time? Three dots > Templates > pick it. Sent in 5 seconds.
>
> **CTA:** "Tomorrow — schedule emails so clients get them at the right time."

### Day 3: Schedule Send
> **Hook:** "It's 9pm. You don't want to email a client now. But you'll forget tomorrow."
>
> Write the email. Don't hit Send.
> Click the little arrow NEXT to the Send button.
> Pick "Schedule send" > tomorrow 9am.
> It goes out at 9am. You look professional. You can go to sleep.
>
> **CTA:** "Tomorrow — snooze emails that aren't urgent yet."

### Day 4: Snooze Emails
> **Hook:** "This email matters but not today. Make it come back later."
>
> Hover over the email. See the clock icon on the right? Click it.
> Pick "Tomorrow", "Next week", or a custom date.
> The email disappears — then pops back into your inbox on that day.
> It's like a reminder that you didn't have to set up.
>
> **CTA:** "Tomorrow — search like a pro."

### Day 5: Search Operators
> **Hook:** "Find any email in 3 seconds. Even from 6 months ago."
>
> In the Gmail search bar, type:
> from:shane — all emails from Shane
> has:attachment — only emails with files
> subject:discovery — only emails about discovery
> older_than:7d — only emails from more than a week ago
>
> Combine them: from:shane has:attachment
> You'll find anything instantly.
>
> **CTA:** "Monday — Google Calendar tricks."

## WEEK 2: CALENDAR — "Your schedule runs itself"

### Day 6: Quick Event Creation
> **Hook:** "Book a meeting in 10 seconds. Not 2 minutes."
>
> Open Google Calendar. Click on the time slot you want.
> Type the name: "Discovery - John Smith".
> Click "More options" only if you need to add location or notes.
> Otherwise just hit Save. Done.
> Or even faster — just type in the search bar: "Discovery with John Thursday 2pm"
>
> **CTA:** "Tomorrow — colour-coding so you can see your week at a glance."

### Day 7: Colour Code Your Events
> **Hook:** "Your calendar is a wall of blue. Let's fix that."
>
> Right-click any event. Pick a colour:
> - Red = Discovery meetings (high priority)
> - Green = Callbacks
> - Yellow = Internal / team stuff
> - Grey = Personal
>
> Now you can glance at your week and instantly know what kind of day it is.
>
> **CTA:** "Tomorrow — stop getting surprised by meetings."

### Day 8: Notifications That Actually Help
> **Hook:** "You missed a meeting because the reminder was 10 minutes before. Fix that."
>
> Click the gear icon > Settings > Event settings.
> Change default notification to 30 minutes.
> For discoveries, open the event and add a SECOND notification: 1 day before.
> Now you get a heads-up the night before AND 30 min before.
>
> **CTA:** "Tomorrow — sharing your calendar so booking doesn't require 5 texts."

### Day 9: Share Your Calendar
> **Hook:** "Shane needs to see your availability. Stop texting back and forth."
>
> Settings > your calendar > "Share with specific people".
> Add Shane's email. Set permission to "See all event details".
> Done. He can see when you're free. No more "are you available Tuesday?"
> This is how the Notion calendar sync works too — it reads from here.
>
> **CTA:** "Tomorrow — the one Google Drive trick that saves everything."

### Day 10: Google Drive Quick Access
> **Hook:** "Every client file. One place. Found in 2 seconds."
>
> Open Google Drive. At the top — "Quick Access" shows your recent files.
> Use the search bar. Type a client name — their folder appears.
> Star important folders (right-click > Add to Starred).
> Starred folders show up in your sidebar. One click access, every time.
>
> **CTA:** "That's your Google Workspace bootcamp. You're faster than 90% of people now."

## BONUS: GEMINI IN WORKSPACE

### Gemini in Gmail — Draft Replies
> **Hook:** "AI writes your email. You just check and send."
>
> Open an email you need to reply to.
> Click "Help me write" (the sparkle icon).
> Type what you want to say in plain English: "Tell them we'll visit Thursday at 3pm to discuss their property options."
> Gemini writes a professional reply. Edit if needed. Send.
> What took 5 minutes now takes 30 seconds.

### Gemini in Docs — Meeting Notes
> **Hook:** "Turn your voice notes into proper documents."
>
> Open Google Docs. Click "Help me write".
> Paste or type your rough notes from a discovery meeting.
> Tell Gemini: "Turn this into a professional client meeting summary."
> Clean, formatted, ready to save to the client folder.
