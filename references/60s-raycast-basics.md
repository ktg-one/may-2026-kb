# 60-Second Training Scripts — Raycast

Target: Kismet staff. Never used a launcher before.
Format: 60 seconds max. Screen recording + voiceover.
Rule: One shortcut, one trick per day.

---

## Day 1: What Is Raycast
> **Hook:** "One keyboard shortcut replaces 10 clicks."
>
> Press Alt + Space. A search bar pops up.
> Type anything — an app name, a file, a Google Doc.
> Press Enter. It opens instantly.
> That's it. Alt + Space is your new best friend.
> Never dig through the Start Menu again.
>
> **CTA:** "Tomorrow — opening Notion in 2 seconds flat."

## Day 2: Launch Any App Instantly
> **Hook:** "Watch this. Alt + Space, type 'not', Enter."
>
> Notion opens. Instantly.
> Try it: Alt + Space, type "cal" — Google Calendar.
> Alt + Space, type "chrome" — your browser.
> Alt + Space, type "out" — Outlook.
> You'll never click a desktop icon again.
>
> **CTA:** "Tomorrow — searching Google without opening a browser first."

## Day 3: Quick Google Search
> **Hook:** "You need to look something up. Don't open Chrome first."
>
> Alt + Space.
> Just type your question. Like "Perth weather" or "ABN lookup".
> Press Enter — it searches Google directly.
> Saves you 3 clicks and 5 seconds every single time.
>
> **CTA:** "Tomorrow — finding that file you lost."

## Day 4: Find Any File
> **Hook:** "That PDF from last week? Found in 3 seconds."
>
> Alt + Space.
> Type part of the filename. Even just "kismet" or "authority".
> It shows matching files on your computer.
> Arrow down to the right one, Enter. Done.
> Never dig through folders again.
>
> **CTA:** "Tomorrow — clipboard history will change your life."

## Day 5: Clipboard History
> **Hook:** "You copied something 10 minutes ago. You need it again."
>
> Alt + Space, type "clipboard".
> Every single thing you've copied today — it's all here.
> Click the one you want. It's pasted.
> Phone numbers, addresses, notes — you'll never lose a copy again.
>
> **CTA:** "That's your Raycast week. Alt + Space for everything."
