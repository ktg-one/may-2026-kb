# 60-Second Training Scripts — Kismet Notion CRM

Target: Field consultants. Zero Notion experience. One skill per day.
Format: 60 seconds max. Screen recording + voiceover.
Rule: Show ONLY what they need to touch. No features, no tours.

---

## WEEK 1: THE BASICS — "I can find my stuff"

### Day 1: The DQ Workspace
> **Hook:** "This is where every lead starts."
>
> Open Notion. Click "DQ Workspace" in the sidebar.
> This is your home base. Every lead you generate starts here.
> You'll see a link to the Fillout DQ Form — that's what you fill at the door.
> Once submitted, the response lands in DQ Response automatically.
> You don't move anything. The system does it.
>
> **CTA:** "Tomorrow — how the DQ Form works."

### Day 2: Filling the DQ Form (Fillout)
> **Hook:** "You're at the door. They're interested. Pull up this form."
>
> Open the DQ Form link from DQ Workspace (bookmark it on your phone).
> Fill in: Name, Phone, Address, Marital Status, basic financial questions.
> The two questions that matter most: Do they own an investment property? Is their super above 100k?
> Hit Submit.
> It goes straight into DQ Response in Notion. No retyping. No paper.
>
> **CTA:** "Tomorrow — what happens after you submit."

### Day 3: How Leads Get Routed
> **Hook:** "You submitted the form. Here's what the system does next."
>
> The system reads two things: investment property owned and super above 100k.
> If both tick yes — that lead goes to WARM LEAD automatically.
> Everyone else goes to Future Prospects.
> You don't decide this. The routing is instant.
> Your job: fill the form accurately. The system does the sorting.
>
> **CTA:** "Tomorrow — what happens with Warm Leads."

### Day 4: The Callback
> **Hook:** "You've got a Warm Lead. Now you call them back."
>
> Check your Warm Leads in DQ Response.
> These are the ones the system flagged as qualified.
> Call them back. The goal: book a discovery meeting.
> If they say yes — book it. The next script shows you how.
> If they say no or not yet — they stay in the pipeline for follow-up.
>
> **CTA:** "Tomorrow — booking a discovery."

### Day 5: Booking a Discovery
> **Hook:** "They said yes. Here's what happens next — and it's fast."
>
> When you book a discovery, the system auto-pushes the person, meeting time, and details straight into the Discovery Workspace.. Shane sees it immediately. No handoff email. No Slack message. It just appears. > Shane takes it from here — he walks into the discovery meeting with everything he needs.
> Your job ended when you booked it. The automation handled the rest.
> 
> Let's configure your calendar to make sure you are with the team! In the sidebar, scroll to the bottom and click on calendar...... now make sure you have Team Kismet Calendar showing on your left and make 
	that the default... now your ready
> >
> **CTA:** "Monday — what Shane does in the discovery and why it matters to you."

## WEEK 2: THE FULL LOOP — "I understand where my leads go"

### Day 6: The Discovery Meeting (Shane's Side)
> **Hook:** "You booked the meeting. Here's what Shane does with it."
>
> Shane opens Discovery Workspace and sees the meeting you booked.
> He goes to the client with the Client Assessment Form.
> He fills it out WITH the client during the meeting — their goals, financial situation, everything.
> You don't touch this part. But knowing it exists means you know your lead isn't lost.
>
> **CTA:** "Tomorrow — what fires after the assessment."

### Day 7: The DocuSign Welcome Email
> **Hook:** "Shane submits the assessment. Then this happens automatically."
>
> The moment Shane submits the Client Assessment Form, a DocuSign Welcome Email fires to the client's email.
> No one clicks send. No one drafts anything. It just goes.
> The client gets their welcome pack and knows the engagement is real.
> Then they wait for the financial analysis before the next step.
>
> **CTA:** "Tomorrow — the two pathways."

### Day 8: SMSF or Property — The Two Pathways
> **Hook:** "After the financial analysis, every client goes one of two directions."
>
> Once the analysis is done, Shane directs the client to either:
> **SMSF** — Self-Managed Super Fund pathway
> **PROPERTY** — Direct property investment pathway
> This decision comes from the assessment data and the financial analysis.
> The system tracks which pathway each client is on — nothing gets lost.
>
> **CTA:** "Tomorrow — what NOT to do."

### Day 9: The 3 Things That Break Everything
> **Hook:** "These three mistakes make the whole system useless."
>
> 1. **Don't half-fill the DQ Form.** If investment property or super fields are blank, the routing breaks. Fill every field.
>
> 2. **Don't skip the callback.** Warm Leads go cold fast. The system flagged them for a reason — call them.
>
> 3. **Don't manually move leads between workspaces.** The automation handles routing. If something looks wrong, tell Kevin. Don't "fix" it yourself.
>
> Follow these and the system works FOR you, not against you.
>
> **CTA:** "Tomorrow — the full flow in 60 seconds."

### Day 10: The Whole Flow in 60 Seconds
> **Hook:** "Here's everything, start to finish, in one breath."
>
> DQ Workspace — you open the DQ Form link.
> You fill it at the door. Submit.
> It lands in DQ Response. System routes: investment property + super above 100k = Warm Lead. Rest = Future Prospects.
> You call back Warm Leads. Book a discovery.
> Person, time, and details auto-push to Discovery Workspace.
> Shane meets the client, fills the Client Assessment Form.
> Submit triggers DocuSign Welcome Email to the client.
> Client waits for financial analysis.
> Shane directs them: SMSF or Property.
> Every step after your DQ Form fires in under a minute.
> Your job: fill the form right, make the callback, book the meeting. Automation does the rest.
>
> **CTA:** "That's your two weeks. You now know the whole pipeline."

## BONUS / AS NEEDED

### Future Prospects — They're Not Dead
> **Hook:** "Just because they didn't qualify now doesn't mean never."
>
> Future Prospects are leads that didn't tick both boxes — investment property AND super above 100k.
> They stay in the system. They get revisited.
> Don't treat Future Prospects as wasted effort. Circumstances change — super grows, properties get bought.
> If a Future Prospect calls back and now qualifies, re-submit a DQ Form with updated info. The system re-routes.

### Phone vs Desktop — What Works Where
> **Hook:** "You're in the field. Here's what works on your phone."
>
> On your phone:
> - DQ Form via Fillout (bookmark it)
> - Check DQ Response for your Warm Leads
> - Make callbacks
>
> Save for desktop:
> - Discovery Workspace overview
> - Anything with lots of reading or typing
>
> 80% of your job works from your phone. Use it.
