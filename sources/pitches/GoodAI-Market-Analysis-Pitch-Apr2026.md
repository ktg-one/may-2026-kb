## 1. Market Context Update: What's Changed Since the Feasibility Report

### 1.1 AI Adoption — The Numbers Have Moved

The feasibility report cited 62% SME automation adoption. Updated Q1 2026 data shows the picture is more nuanced and more urgent:

- **64% of Australian SMBs** now use AI regularly (daily/weekly/monthly), up from 39% in mid-2024 — a 64% increase in under two years ([AI Lab Australia](https://www.ailabaustralia.com/blog/ai-adoption-australian-smbs-2026))
- **But only 5% are "AI-enabled"** — meaning integrated into core operations and strategy, not just experimenting with ChatGPT ([ScaleSuite](https://www.scalesuite.com.au/resources/ai-adoption-in-australian-smes))
- **Adoption by size**: 82% for 200-500 employees, 68% for 20-199, 40% for 5-19, under 33% for micro-businesses ([Department of Industry](https://www.industry.gov.au/publications/ai-adoption-tracker))
- **Economic impact**: AI is adding $21B annually to Australian GDP, projected to reach $142B by 2030 (Tech Council of Australia)
- **The gap that matters**: 95% still report zero measurable ROI (MIT study, unchanged). The adoption rate went up; the success rate didn't.

**What this means for the pitch**: The market has moved from "should we adopt AI?" to "why isn't our AI working?" — which is exactly the problem Good AI + Kismet solve. The 5% "AI-enabled" figure is the real number. The other 59% who are "using AI" but not getting results are the addressable market.

### 1.2 The Talent Squeeze — Still Acute, Getting Worse

From the original AI Talent Gap research, the structural shortage persists:

- 1M+ tech workers needed by 2030; 78% of tech leaders view AI/ML as the defining 2026 trend
- Perth automation engineer mean salary: $134K (12% above national average)
- "Business Analyst translators" remain the most acute shortage — the people who bridge tech and business strategy
- 44% of qualified permanent migrants work below skill level — $43K per person in lost productivity
- Only 18% of employees who received AI training feel independently prepared

**Net effect on Perth SMEs**: They can't hire their way out. The local talent market is both expensive and thin. External advisory and managed implementation are premium services by necessity, not choice.

## 2. Canva AI: What It Means for SME Marketing Automation

### 2.1 The 2026 Canva AI Landscape

Canva is no longer a template editor. As of April 2026, it's a full AI design-and-marketing suite with over 25 integrated AI tools under the Magic Studio umbrella. Key developments:

**Foundational shift (late 2025):** Canva launched its own design model — trained on design layers and formats, not flat images. When you generate with Canva AI, you get editable layered designs, not static pictures you have to rebuild. This is a material capability upgrade for SMEs who lack design staff. ([Canva Magic Studio](https://www.canva.com/canva-ai/))

**April 2026 acquisitions:** Canva acquired Simtheory (AI collaboration/agent management) and Ortto (customer data and marketing automation). Combined with earlier acquisitions of MagicBrief, MangoAI, and Doohly, Canva now covers the full marketing lifecycle: ideation → creation → deployment → measurement → optimisation. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/08/canva-doubles-down-on-ai-and-marketing-automation-with-simtheory-ortto-acquisitions/))

**Key AI features for SMEs:**
- Magic Design: prompt-to-presentation/social-post generation using OpenAI API + Canva's 100M+ asset library
- AI Social Media Post Generator: auto-generates on-brand posts with hashtags, emojis, multi-format adaptation
- Brand Kit integration: AI enforces brand fonts, colors, tone across all generated content
- Magic Animate: adds motion to still images (new early 2026)
- Real-time credit tracker (March 2026) for usage monitoring

**Pricing:** Free tier available. Pro at $13/month ($120/year). Enterprise with unlimited AI credits and Canva Shield (privacy/security/indemnification) for 100+ seats.

### 2.2 What Canva AI Means for the Good AI Pitch

Canva AI is not a competitor — it's a **component** in the automation stack and a **proof point** for the pitch:

1. **It validates the market**: If Canva is investing hundreds of millions in SME marketing automation, the demand signal is confirmed. Perth SMEs are already paying for Canva Pro. They just don't know how to connect it to the rest of their workflow.

2. **It creates the "last mile" problem**: Canva generates content. But it doesn't schedule it across platforms, respond to reviews, nurture leads, or connect to Xero. That's the integration layer Good AI sells — n8n orchestration connecting Canva outputs to Buffer/Hootsuite scheduling, Google Business Profile management, and CRM routing.

3. **It anchors a pricing comparison**: Canva Pro costs $156/year. A social media manager in Perth costs $82K-$137K/year. Good AI's AI Social Multiplier ($1,500 build + $180/mo retainer) sits between them — 10x cheaper than hiring, but integrated into business operations in a way Canva alone can't achieve.

This section builds the economic argument for automation over hiring. It's the single most persuasive comparison for owner-operators.

**True cost of a hire** (beyond salary): Add 12% super ($7,800-$18,480), workers comp (~1.5%), payroll tax (if over threshold), recruitment fees (15-20% of salary = $12,000-$30,000 one-off), leave loading, equipment, training. Plus the time cost: managing a social media hire takes 3-5 hours/week of the owner's time for briefing, approval, and direction. At an owner's opportunity cost of $75-$150/hr, that's an additional $900-$3,000/month in hidden cost.

### 3.4 The Hybrid Model Recommendation

For a typical Perth SME (5-50 employees, $1M-$10M revenue):

**Phase 1 — Automate the foundation ($260-$600/mo)**
Deploy Canva AI + Buffer + n8n automation for content generation, scheduling, review management. Owner or existing staff reviews AI output 30 min/day. Eliminates the need for a dedicated social hire.

**Phase 2 — Add fractional human expertise ($1,000-$2,500/mo)**
Once automation is running, engage a fractional social media strategist for 5-10 hours/month. Their job: quarterly strategy, content calendar planning, brand voice refinement, campaign concepts. The automation handles execution.

**Phase 3 — Scale with intelligence ($500-$850/mo retainer)**
Upgrade to Good AI Enterprise tier. Deploy agentic workflows: AI monitors competitor activity, adjusts posting schedules based on engagement data, generates weekly performance reports, auto-escalates issues.

**Result**: Full social media operation at $1,760-$3,950/month vs. $9,200-$12,500/month for a full-time hire. That's a **60-80% cost reduction** with comparable or better consistency and 24/7 availability.
