## 🔄 Lead Progression Workflow — Operations 2026

DO NOT TOUCH DQ FORMS OR CLIENT ASSESSMENT DATABASE IN THE DATABASE FOLDER

### Purpose

Step-by-step guide for processing leads from entry to client onboarding.

---

### **Step 1: Fresh Lead Enters**

✅ **What happens:** New lead submits DQ Form and enters the system? —> [DQ Form Responses](https://www.notion.so/0761ca2fe7bf4613822a57d96625863c?pvs=21) 

✅ **Where it goes:** DQ Form Responses database  —> [Pipeline 2026](https://www.notion.so/302772b128b0808ba849c1b2210bac78?pvs=21) 

✅ **Initial status:** LEAD → DQ FORM → QUALIFIED LEAD

---

### **Step 2: [CHECK** *(Click Me) - Verify it landed correctly*](https://www.notion.so/302772b128b0808ba849c1b2210bac78?pvs=21)

✅ **Action:** Open the **CHECK** view in your Operations 2026 database

✅ **Verify:**

- Lead shows up in the database
- Phone number is valid (not fake)
- All required fields are filled
- Lead owner is assigned

✅ **If problems:** Mark as FAKE NUMBER or add notes about issues

---

### **Step 3: FEEDBACK CALL** *(Feedback Call + Discovery Booked)*

✅ **Action:** Schedule discovery meeting on calendar

✅ **Then update:**

- Fill in **Discovery Booked** date field
- Change status to PROSPECT
- Booking in Calendar will trigger Client Assessment Form

---

### **Step 4: Automate to Client Assessment Form**

✅ **Trigger:** System automatically triggers when:

- Discovery Booked date is set AND
- Status = PROSPECT

✅ **Result:** Assessment form is generated and queued

---

### **Step 5: Consultant Fills Extra Form**

✅ **Consultant does:**

- Reviews discovery call notes
- Completes additional assessment fields
- Adds SMSF or Property pathway notes
- Tags special requirements

✅ **Then:** Update status to ONBOARDING

---

### **Step 6: Append to the Client**

✅ **System consolidates:**

- DQ Form responses
- Discovery call notes
- Consultant assessment
- Pathway preferences
- Next steps

✅ **Result:** Complete client record ready for onboarding

---

### **Step 7: Client Receives Email**

✅ **Automated email sent with:**

- Welcome message
- Pathway summary (SMSF or Property)
- Next steps and timeline
- Required documents checklist
- Case manager contact details

✅ **Final status:** Update to **ONBOARDED** or **CLIENT**

---

## 🎯 Quick Status Flow

**LEAD** → **QUALIFIED** → **PROSPECT** → **ONBOARDED** → **CLIENT**

---

## 📍 Key Views to Use

- **CHECK** — Verify new leads landed correctly
- **FEEDBACK CALL** — Qualified leads ready for discovery
- **Discovery** — Pipeline board view by status
- **Active Pipeline** — All active work

---

## ⚠️ Remember

- Always verify leads in CHECK view first
- Discovery must be booked before assessment form
- Consultant must review before final email sends
- Use Notes field for any issues or exceptions
