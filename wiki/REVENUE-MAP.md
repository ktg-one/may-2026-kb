# Revenue Map

Single-source view of all [[good-ai]] revenue streams, status, and blockers.

## 1. Direct SME Automation Builds

One-time build + managed retainer model.

| Tier | Build Price | Retainer/mo |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| Starter | $1,500-$3,500 | $150-$400 |
| Growth | $3,000-$5,500 | $300-$550 |
| Enterprise | $5,500-$8,500 | $550-$850 |

- Avg build: $4,500 (direct), $7,000 (channel-sourced)
- Gross margin: 75% builds, 85% retainers
- Client LTV (18mo): $15,000-$25,000
- ROI for clients: 391-596% Year 1, 1.7-2.4mo payback
- **Status: PRE-REVENUE.** No completed implementation builds. No published case studies.
- **Blocker:** Need first paying client + published case study.

## 2. Channel Partnerships

[[wiki/clients/kismet-finance]] is the first and only active channel partner.

- Kismet: 4 BDMs, 198 leads, ~49 warm

- Per-partner economics: $5K setup, 6-15 leads/yr, $8K-$20K revenue per client Year 1
- Target: 3-4 channel partners by Year 2
- **Status: Kismet ACTIVE.** No other partners signed.
- **Blocker:** Need first conversion through Kismet [[PIPELINE]].
