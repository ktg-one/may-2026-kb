# Trillet AI Pty Ltd

Australian Pty Ltd. The voice operating layer that [[wiki/good-ai|Good AI Australia]] is launching its productised voice-agent service on. Founder relationship; partnership terms.

## Profile

- **Entity:** Trillet AI Pty Ltd — Australian Pty Ltd
- **Tagline:** "The voice operating layer."
- **What it is:** A productised, white-label-able voice-AI platform that runs the [[wiki/concepts/voice-operating-layer|Verify · Ground · Act · Follow-through]] capability model.
- **Scale signal:** in production with 1,200+ businesses; 1M+ live customer calls handled (per Trillet site, May 2026).
- **Customer logos visible on site:** Aargon Debt Collection, Taylor Rose, HRCovered, CommsChannel, dd-fs, Strata Blue, BarkBox, Autopay, ConsumerAffairs, Rime, Who Needs Leads, MetaVoice, Go To Court.

## Compliance & certifications

- **SOC 2 Type II**
- **ISO 27001**
- **HIPAA** (HIPAA-ready workflows)
- **GDPR**
- **TCPA** (Telephone Consumer Protection Act compliance)
- **Configurable data residency by geography** (supports Australian residency — the basis for [[wiki/good-ai|Good AI's]] PRIS Act alignment)
- **99.97% stability SLA** (published)
- Encryption in transit + at rest; automated PII redaction; SSO; role-based access; 90-day audit log

## Pricing (Good AI's relevant tiers)

| Plan | Cost | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Basic** | $49/month | 1 AI agent, 1 workspace, 1 phone number, 150 included minutes |
| **Studio** ⭐ Good AI launches here | $99/month flat | Unlimited AI agents, **3 phone numbers**, 100 included minutes, up to 3 sub-accounts (+$15/each), full white-label branding, all integrations, Meta/FB lead form integration, priority support |
| **Agency** ⭐ Good AI scales here | $299/month flat | 10 phone numbers, 300 included minutes, unlimited sub-accounts, custom domain, custom branded emails, custom minute markup, dedicated support |

**Overage rates:** $5/month per additional phone number (all tiers). $0.20/minute (Basic) or $0.12/minute (Studio/Agency).

**No per-call platform fees, no per-seat fees.** The flat plan absorbs LLM, voice (ElevenLabs), transcription and telephony into the single per-minute overage rate.

**28-day money-back guarantee. No contracts.**

## Telephony model

Clients keep their existing business number and forward unanswered calls to a Trillet-provided number. No number change visible to the client's customers. Setup is ~30 seconds at the carrier level.

## Good AI's relationship

- Founder ([[wiki/entities/kevin-tan|Kev]]) introduced to Trillet after they saw his prompt-engineering work.
- Good AI is on a partnership / pay-as-you-go arrangement; launching on the **Studio plan ($99/month flat)** for the SEA program.
- Will move to **Agency plan ($299/month)** once client volume justifies the unlimited sub-accounts + custom domain.

## Migration contingency

Good AI's voice-agent logic, prompts and flows are kept platform-agnostic. Estimated 2–4 weeks to migrate to an alternative provider (e.g. Bland.ai) if Trillet's pricing, performance or service became untenable. Given Trillet's current certification posture, stability SLA and pricing transparency, this contingency is unlikely to be invoked.
