# Kevin Tan

Founder and operator of [[wiki/good-ai|Good AI Australia]]. Based in Perth, Western Australia. Sole trader establishing under the Self-Employment Assistance (SEA) program.

Email: kevin.pl.tan@gmail.com

## Credentials (substantiable)

### Prompt-engineering rankings

- **PromptBase — top 0.8% (September 2025).** Ranking achieved after delivering a production agentic workflow to a San Francisco company. PromptBase listing is the supporting evidence.
- **Recursive Council — top 0.01% (December 2025).** Admission earned by passing a self-designed adversarial benchmark against GCP Vertex AI's prompt iterator (a two-year iterative effort). The benchmark prompt was published across Reddit prompt-engineering threads for three weeks seeking peer-level responses (~25,000 views, one pass returned), which led directly to Recursive Council admission. The 0.01% intrinsic position was derived from a ThoughtLogic-style enumeration of the global prompt-engineering population at the time — counting AI users → prompter subset → prompt-creator subset, including universities, independent engineers, prompt-marketplace listees and red-teamers; excluding frontier-lab employees and in-house model engineers; normalised against competition-derived rates. The adversarial prompt is on file in the vault as evidence.
