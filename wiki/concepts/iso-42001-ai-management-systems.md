# ISO/IEC 42001 — AI Management Systems

International management-system standard for the responsible governance, risk management, and ethical use of AI. Published December 2023 by ISO/IEC. The AI counterpart to ISO 27001 (information security management) and ISO 9001 (quality management). Held personally by [[wiki/entities/kevin-tan|Kev]] as part of [[wiki/good-ai|Good AI Australia's]] compliance posture, complementing [[wiki/entities/trillet-ai|Trillet AI's]] organisational SOC 2 Type II / ISO 27001 / HIPAA / GDPR / TCPA certifications.

## What it covers

ISO/IEC 42001 specifies requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining and continually improving an *AI management system* within an organisation. It targets entities that provide or use AI-based products or services and aims to ensure that AI is developed and operated responsibly and trustworthily. Core themes:

- **Governance** — organisational accountability for AI outcomes; defined roles and responsibilities; documented policies.
- **Risk management** — AI-specific risk identification (bias, fairness, transparency, safety, privacy, security, robustness, accountability).
- **AI lifecycle controls** — controls across the full lifecycle: planning, data acquisition, model development, deployment, operation, monitoring, retirement.
- **Transparency & accountability** — documented decision-making processes, system inventories, impact assessments, incident handling.
- **Continual improvement** — internal audits, management reviews, corrective action — same management-system shape as ISO 27001 / ISO 9001.

## Why it matters for Good AI

- **Practitioner-level compliance backing.** [[wiki/entities/trillet-ai|Trillet]] holds the platform-level certifications (SOC 2 II, ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR, TCPA, configurable Australian residency); ISO/IEC 42001 in Kev's hands adds the *AI governance* layer that those security standards don't directly address. The pairing is differentiating in the WA SME segment where no competitor currently offers either layer.
- **WA PRIS Act alignment.** The Privacy and Responsible Information Sharing (PRIS) Act 2024 (WA) emphasises responsible information handling and transparency. ISO/IEC 42001 alignment is direct evidence of the governance practices the Act would expect.
- **Procurement signal.** Larger clients (financial services, healthcare-adjacent, government-adjacent) increasingly ask AI vendors for governance evidence. Citing ISO/IEC 42001 in advisory and proposal material is a credibility floor.
