## Why Perth specifically

- **Digital parenting penetration** — 67% of Australian families use mobile wallets / digital payments by 2025.
- **Education spend** — Perth is Australia's #2 most expensive city for education; families spend AUD 122,106 over 13 years of public school (35% YoY rise).
- **Personalized gift market growth** — Little Dance, LilyCraft, Givi Gifts trending up in Perth.
- **Government digital adoption** — 50K WA Student Assistance Payment claims in 24 hours; 130K+ families on ServiceWA.

## Target personas (from source)

- **"Grace" — Digital-Savvy Perth Parent.** Female, 28-38, HHI AUD 65K-120K, kids 2-8. Values convenience, embraces tech, gift-giving focused. Uses ServiceWA, shops Paperbird Books Fremantle.
- **"Ben" — Value-Conscious Tech Adopter.** 25-42, HHI AUD 45K-85K, kids 3-10. Budget-conscious, comparison shops. Uses Spriggy; shops Dymocks / independent bookstores.

## Hypotheses

1. Perth parents will pay AUD 7.99-15.99 for AI face-integrated digital personalized books.
2. Digital-first delivery captures 15-25% preview→purchase conversion.
3. Premium add-ons attach at 35-50%.

## Competitive landscape (from source)

Magic Picture Story, Epic Tales, ChildBook.ai (Canva app). All AI-generated personalized children's books.

## Acquisition channels suggested

- **Grace:** Facebook/Instagram ads on Perth parenting groups; Google Ads on "personalized children's books Perth"; partnerships with Paperbird Books / Spacecubed.
- **Ben:** YouTube pre-roll on educational kids' content; Perth parenting Facebook groups; referral programs.
