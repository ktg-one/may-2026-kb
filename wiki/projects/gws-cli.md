# Google Workspace CLI

Rust-based CLI covering 18 Google Workspace services. Feature-complete at v0.22.3.

## Services (18)

Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Sheets, Docs, Slides, Forms, Admin, People, Groups, Tasks, Keep, Chat, Meet, Sites, Vault, Classroom, Cloud Identity

## Architecture

- Auth: Device-flow OAuth
- Project folder: `google-workspace-cli/`
