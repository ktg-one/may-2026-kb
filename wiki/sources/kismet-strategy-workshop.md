## The two pivotal theses

### 1. The 2026 Perth Economic Anomaly

> [!danger]
> **Confluence of constraints:** severe labor scarcity (extreme job vacancy ratio) + acute administrative paralysis from compliance/multi-disciplinary reporting + tightening regulation. Traditional human-heavy scaling models are prohibitively expensive.

### 2. The Personality Bottleneck

> [!warning]
> KFG's high-touch *"chatty salesman"* culture is excellent for front-end conversion but exhibits **strict linear capacity limits** — growth is restricted by physical/temporal capacity of key relationship managers. Referral partners also face document-gathering friction that caps the volume of vetted leads they can accept.

**Pivot mandate:** from personality-driven sales consultancy → tech-enabled platform delivering application-ready clients with zero administrative friction.

## Three-phase workshop architecture

| Phase | Activity | Tooling |
|-------|----------|---------|
| **1. Pre-workshop intelligence** | Public footprint analysis, document ingestion, Meta ad library reverse-engineering, synthesis into Intelligence Brief | **DIVE v8.0 KTG ULTRA prompt** + interactive audio synthesis |
| **2. Live workshop** | Vulnerability scan, Disruption Mapping, **Domain Persona Play**, "Synthetic Participant as Trigger Engine" | Multi-agent swarm orchestration (Compliance / Strategy / Technical agents debating live) |
| **3. Strategy formulation** | Architectural revelation — escape "Capital Expenditure Trap"; mandate **decoupled UI ↔ data layer** | Make.com as exclusive integration layer (chosen over Zapier for "supremacy" — Tier 2 logic) |

## Concepts to track

- **Corporate Facilitator role** — specialized hybrid of instructional design + change enablement + session leadership.
- **Shock Fact Framework** — psychological tool that cross-references intel-brief facts against executive assumptions in real time.
- **Capital Expenditure Trap** — SMEs over-investing in custom builds. Counter: lightweight relational data + visual logic + dashboard composition.
- **The Three-Layer architecture** —
  - **Relational Brain** — flexible data layer, separates People from Deals
  - **Visual Logic Nerves** — Make.com (exclusive)
  - **Control Room Face** — centralized admin dashboard

> [!note] Historical parallel
> MIT 1998: a sociable robot named "Kismet" was built to express human-like emotions and study artificial empathy. KFG attempts the same — scaling empathetic human sales culture through digital architecture.
