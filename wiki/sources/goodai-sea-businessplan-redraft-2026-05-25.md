# Good AI Australia — SEA Business Plan Redraft (25 May 2026)

Source summary of the end-to-end rewrite of the Good AI Australia Self-Employment Assistance (SEA) business plan, completed 25 May 2026 across this session. Compiled against the Oct/Nov 2025 SEA scaffold, the Apr 2026 funding pitch + market analysis, the live Trillet AI platform site, the Darling Good voice persona, and three rounds of clarification from Kev.

## What this document commits to

- **Offering:** Productised AI voice agents for Perth SMEs, delivered as a managed service on the [[wiki/entities/trillet-ai|Trillet AI]] white-label platform. Seven customer-facing subscription tiers ($149–$499/month) plus add-on services.
- **Two routes to market, run in parallel:** direct-to-SME subscriptions AND white-label resale to other Perth agencies/consultants. Market signal decides which is the lead lucrative path.
- **Target market:** service-oriented SMEs, 1–50 employees, South Perth + adjacent suburbs. Secondary segment: micro-businesses / sole traders (1–3 people).
- **Y1 target:** 60 active subscribers, $15K–$20K MRR by Month 12. Break-even Month 4–5.
- **Y2–Y3 trajectory:** 100+ clients / $200K annual revenue (Y2); $300K+ (Y3); evaluate sole trader → Pty Ltd.
- **Compliance posture:** PRIS-aligned via [[wiki/entities/trillet-ai|Trillet's]] certified posture (SOC 2 II + ISO 27001 + HIPAA + GDPR + TCPA + configurable Australian data residency), reinforced by Kev's personal [[wiki/concepts/iso-42001-ai-management-systems|ISO/IEC 42001]] qualification.
- **Operating policy:** stay fluid — no long-range platform lock-in, no over-planned roadmaps.
