## Core thesis

> [!quote] The strategic vulnerability
> Kismet's revenue is **not limited by its own sales team's ability to generate leads**; it is constrained by its partners' capacity to *process* those leads. Administrative bottlenecks at referral partners act as a systemic throttle on Kismet's revenue.

**Therefore:** Kismet's most effective growth strategy is not to send more leads, but to send **better leads** — application-ready ones. Good AI's role is to build the AI-driven platform that solves admin bottlenecks **for Kismet's partners**, transforming Kismet from a replaceable lead source into an indispensable tech-enabled platform.

## Key concepts introduced

- **B2B Referral Hub model** — Kismet is a high-touch super-aggregator, not direct-to-consumer.
- **"Application-ready" client** — the unit of value delivered to referral partners.
- **AI-MELD** — the Good AI × Kismet integration framing (this doc's namesake).
