# Purpose

The "soul" of this vault. Read at the start of every ingest and query. Update when direction shifts (it will).

## Why this vault exists

To serve as the **single coherent operating system** for Kev's commercial and intellectual work — across all clients, all internal builds, and all AI agents that touch his life. Built on the LLM Wiki pattern (Karpathy, [nashsu/llm_wiki](https://github.com/nashsu/llm_wiki)) because that pattern is proven to cancel context loss and drift across sessions, models and tools. The vault + the auto-memory layer together *are* Claude's (and any other AI's) persistent state with Kev.
